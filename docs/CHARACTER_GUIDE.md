## 🎬 Animation Frame Guidelines

- **Directions Supported**: 8 Directions (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW)
- **Idle State**: 4 Frames (Breathes/Bobs vertically)
- **Walk / Move State**: 8 Frames (Smooth stride)
- **Attack / Click Action**: 6 Frames (Impact frame with particle trail)
- **Hit / Hurt State**: 2 Frames (Flash white + recoil)
- **Death / Defeat State**: 8 Frames (Dissolve into gold & soul particles)

---

## 🌌 Universe Context & Game Mechanics Alignment

- **3 Core Realms**:
  1. **Monster Arena (⚔️ Chiến Đấu Quái Vật)**: High-intensity boss clicks, red/purple hues.
  2. **Lumberyard (🪵 Tiều Phu Chặt Gỗ)**: Forest nature realm, green/brown hues.
  3. **Crystal Mine (🪨 Thợ Mỏ Khai Thác)**: Deep underground ore cave, amber/cyan/gold hues.

- **Design Philosophy**:
  - **Silhouette First**: Every character must have an instantly identifiable silhouette from a distance.
  - **Color Cohesion**: Characters share saturated fantasy tones with 2-3 accent primary colors.
  - **No Copycat**: All designs are 100% original custom concepts tailored strictly for *Tap Tap Clicker Multiplayer*.
